HARBORLINE SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Parties
This Services Agreement is entered into by Harborline Studio LLC (Client) and Northstar Data Works Ltd. (Provider).

2. Effective Date and Term
This Agreement starts on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027.

3. Payment Terms
Client will pay Provider USD 4,500 per month. Invoices are payable within 30 calendar days of receipt.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice.

5. Limitation of Liability
Each party’s aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at fees paid in the preceding six months, except for fraud or willful misconduct.

6. Confidentiality
Each party must keep the other party's confidential information private during the Agreement and for two years after it ends.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
